# UberPokmi

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Lucide. Frontend only, as requested in the supplied master brief. Local implementation substitutes for unavailable Gemini Canvas.

## Users

Malaysian consumers booking rides, ordering meals and sending parcels; investor-demo and usability-test participants.

## Product Purpose

Complete a simulated transaction, track it, and find its receipt in unified Activity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Three complete local journeys. No backend, real payments, authentication, GPS, dispatch, messages or secret API keys. Fictional estimates, people and merchants. Local persistence and reset. English UI.

## Brand Commitments

uberpokmi. — Your city. Your way. User-pinned Urban Velocity dark palette, lime accent, Space Grotesk and Inter; premium useful app with sidebar and mobile bottom navigation.

## Evidence on Hand

User master implementation prompt. No existing application or supplied brand assets. All booking data is demonstration content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard controls, semantic forms, accessible dialogs, readable contrast, reduced motion and responsive layouts from 375px.

## Implementation assumptions

Code-first React implementation, hosted as a static Vercel frontend at uberpokmi.syahmiaof.my. Stylized schematic map remains available offline; optional OpenStreetMap layer may use network. Pricing and parcel limits are explicitly fictional.
